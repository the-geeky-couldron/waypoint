# Artwork and map-preview sources

Waypoint's 27 portrait world illustrations in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/art_*` are original images generated for this project with `gpt-image-2` and selected for legibility in the mobile gallery. The retained cover prompts are in [`artwork-prompts-v2.jsonl`](artwork-prompts-v2.jsonl); raw source generations and older unused prompts are excluded from Git. The set uses individual game-inspired art direction for each world, physical brush and print texture, and a clear central subject for small mobile cards. It does not use publisher art, logos, characters, or copied locations.

The POI atlases and destination markers for 36 worlds are original `gpt-image-2` generations. Each has its own prompt, material, color system, framing, and destination motif. Source prompts are kept in [`tools/imagegen/poi-atlases-all.jsonl`](../tools/imagegen/poi-atlases-all.jsonl). [`import_imagegen_poi_atlases.py`](../scripts/import_imagegen_poi_atlases.py) removes the chroma background and creates the app-sized sprites using reviewed row and column bounds in the [`crop manifest`](../tools/imagegen/poi-crop-manifest.json). A review of all 38 day/night atlases found shifted crops, missing or reordered cells, and neighboring fragments. The manifest records 92 tracked cell overrides across 23 ImageGen worlds; 43 use isolated original artwork from [`poi-cell-repairs.jsonl`](../tools/imagegen/poi-cell-repairs.jsonl). [`import_imagegen_poi_cell_repairs.py`](../scripts/import_imagegen_poi_cell_repairs.py) can apply the tracked PNGs without the ignored source sheets. Night POI glows are generated one cell at a time so they cannot spill into adjacent symbols. [Contact sheets](poi-atlas-audit.md) show every final pair.

Machine Atlas (NieR) is the other original-art exception to that ImageGen workflow. Its POI diamonds, monochrome glyphs, and destination targets are deterministically drawn by [`generate_map_assets.py`](../scripts/generate_map_assets.py). Its day palette and map assets are used at every hour.

Night City's 49-category atlas combines seven Cyberpunk 2077 map glyphs used in ten directly matching OpenStreetMap categories with 39 original Waypoint glyphs. Its player caret and destination target are original Waypoint artwork. The source files, URLs, hashes, semantic mapping, and the maintainer's confirmation of written CD PROJEKT RED permission for public app and repository use are documented in the [map icon notes](poi-icon-sources.md). The permission itself is held privately. No game screenshot is packaged as a runtime map asset. The same high-contrast symbols are used against each world's day and night map styles; local-time switching applies to the map palette, materials, and layout.

The current onboarding uses compact world-specific place markers, a horse illustration and two rendered clips. The 27 separate world emblem stickers use [`world-emblem-prompts.jsonl`](world-emblem-prompts.jsonl), with a different motif and material direction for each theme. `tools/waypoint-motion/install_artwork_v2.py` installs the compressed world covers; `install_world_emblems.py` removes the emblem chroma background and preserves the painted outline. `prepare_public_assets.py` stages the few Android images needed to re-render Remotion clips; generated copies remain ignored. Earlier unused sticker and onboarding stills are excluded from the source tree.

The first onboarding Earth image is adapted from NASA Earth Observatory's [Blue Marble western hemisphere](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/) image by Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon. The rotating globe and its country geometry are rendered in Waypoint from geographic data; the globes and motion clips in `tools/waypoint-motion/` are Waypoint compositions.

There are 38 world-card map previews (27 WebP resources under `drawable-nodpi/` and 11 PNG resources under `drawable/`), captured at documented public locations in `WorldPreviewPlaces.kt`. Machine Atlas and Night City were refreshed from a connected Android phone against the current styles; the other cards show earlier captures. The debug-only `WorldPreviewCaptureActivity` can refresh them: capture once for day, once with the `night` intent extra, and Dark Zone with the `hologram` extra; each variant gets a distinct output name. OpenFreeMap supplies OpenMapTiles vector features and OpenStreetMap geography; attribution is shown in Map info and each card identifies its city or landmark.

Referral choices use the recognizable Instagram, Reddit, YouTube, Google, GitHub, and Telegram vector marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/); the Friend mark is a Material icon. These marks identify the named services and do not imply affiliation.

Game names identify the source of stylistic inspiration. Waypoint is independent of those games and their publishers.

The original-art descriptions above apply to the map styles, the 37 wholly original place-symbol atlases, destination markers, player markers, world illustrations, and promotional compositions. Night City's bundled game glyphs are the documented exception. Retained interface and onboarding images have separate origins.
