Five worlds. One route.

Scroll to drive it. The line runs through our five best-looking worlds, all captured on a phone at public London demonstration points. The streets and place data stay real, and only the atlas around them changes.

  1. Old West

    Red Dead Redemption 2 inspired

    Parchment streets, hand-lettered labels and western controls, with woods and meadows drawn in ink and stipple.

  2. Night City

    Cyberpunk 2077 inspired

    Red city blocks, cyan routes and controls that stay sharp at speed. Find location, saved places and recenter are one tap away.

  3. Sheikah Slate

    The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild inspired

    Gold roads over a weathered survey base, with blue and gold exploration controls around the same real streets.

  4. Pixel World

    Minecraft inspired

    Block-style roads, grass and water in pixel art. Search, routes and turn guidance work exactly the same.

  5. Exclusion Zone

    S.T.A.L.K.E.R.: Shadow of Chernobyl inspired

    Added in 2.0 after a community request. Muted survey greens and amber details, with a route line that never gets lost.

The map symbols and destination markers are Waypoint artwork. Map imagery uses real OpenStreetMap-derived data; fictional game locations are not added to real cities.

The map changes with the light.

Every world has day and night artwork. Waypoint switches automatically using your device’s local time, with no network check.

A familiar voice. A different mood.

Every world adds short, original Waypoint phrases to your chosen Android speech voice. The navigation engine still provides distance, turns, road names, rerouting and arrival estimates. Extra phrases are skipped near an imminent turn.

How voice guidance works
Android speech

“In 200 metres, turn left onto King’s Road.”

A short world-specific line can follow when the next turn is not imminent.

Skyrim, Horizon, DOS Atlas and Liberty City also have recorded prompts for some turns. Android speech reads the full instruction when a recording does not match.

Navigation instructions

Same world on the car screen.

Android Auto can show your selected world on supported installs. An APK from GitHub may not appear in a real car’s launcher because Android requires a trusted install source. The image below uses a fixed route in a desktop simulator, not a road test.

Guidance stays legible. The city stays real.

Waypoint shows route progress, reroutes if you go off route and estimates arrival time. It does not include live traffic, offline routing or lane guidance.

Where Waypoint stands.

A hobby app that went public, kept its promises and picked up a few firsts on the way.

  1. Reached No. 12 in Android on Upstar

    Upstar ranks the fastest-rising GitHub projects by topic. Reached on November 7, 2026.

    See the ranking

  2. Featured on Repolode

    Listed alongside other open-source projects worth finding.

    Open the listing

  3. 38 worlds, all drawn from scratch

    Every map style is authored for Waypoint, with its own roads, labels, textures, place symbols and destination markers. Eleven arrived in 2.0, from nautical charts to a 16-bit adventure.

    How the artwork is made

  4. Shaped by the people who use it

    Ideas from Balzamus, matvejsyrygin256-dotcom, 6HentaiMaster9, artmartins28 and VoidKittyKat became quests, new worlds, bicycle navigation and personal place names in 2.0.

    Read the release notes

  5. On the dashboard too

    Android Auto projects your selected world with a full route line, turn cues and a camera that follows travel, and now offers other routes on the car screen.

    Android Auto setup

  6. Free, open and private

    No ads, no paywall and no Waypoint account. The code is GPL-3.0-only, made in India and open to the world.

    View the source

Install Waypoint on Android.

The Android APK is available from Waypoint’s latest GitHub release.

  1. Open the release.

    The download link takes you to the latest Waypoint release on GitHub.

  2. Download the APK.

    Under Assets, download Waypoint.apk.

  3. Install on Android.

    Open the APK and follow Android’s prompts. Saved places and settings stay on your device.
